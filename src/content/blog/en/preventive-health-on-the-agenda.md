Preventive health is the quiet work that keeps a community strong. A screening booked on time, a senior who stays mobile, a child whose asthma is managed before it becomes an emergency: these are the outcomes that reduce suffering and ease pressure on an overstretched system.

A city councillor does not run the health system. Health programs are largely provincial and federal. What a councillor can do is advocacy, literacy, and connection: helping residents find and use the programs and services they are already entitled to, and pressing higher levels of government where local needs go unmet.
