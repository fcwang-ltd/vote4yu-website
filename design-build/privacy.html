Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Who we are

This website is operated by the campaign of Haohui Yu, a candidate for Markham Ward 2 in the 2026 City of Markham municipal election. In this policy, "we," "us," and "the campaign" mean the Haohui Yu Campaign.

We can be reached at dave@vote4yu.ca.

What information we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us, plus basic technical information about your visit.

Information you give us directly:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, postal code or address, when you sign up for updates, volunteer, request a lawn sign, or contact us
  • Any message or comment you send us
  • Information you provide when you make a campaign contribution

Information collected automatically:

  • Your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and how you arrived at this site
  • This is collected through Google Analytics using cookies. A cookie is a small file your browser stores that helps a website remember your visit.

Why we collect it

We use your information to:

  • Send you campaign updates and news, if you asked for them
  • Respond to your questions and messages
  • Organize volunteers, events, and lawn sign requests
  • Process and record campaign contributions, and meet the reporting rules under the Municipal Elections Act, 1996
  • Understand which parts of this website people find useful

We do not use your information for any commercial purpose.

Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

We share it only with:

  • Service providers who run our tools on our behalf and only so they can provide that service to us
  • Government authorities, where we are required by law to disclose it, including campaign finance filings under the Municipal Elections Act, 1996

Some of these providers store data outside Canada, which means that information may be subject to the laws of that country.

We keep your information for the length of the campaign and for as long afterwards as we need it to meet our record-keeping and financial reporting obligations. After that, we securely delete it. Contribution records are kept for the period required by the Municipal Elections Act, 1996.

How we protect it

We use reputable service providers and limit access to personal information to campaign staff and volunteers who need it to do their work. No website can promise perfect security, but we take reasonable steps to protect what you give us.

Your choices

You can ask us to:

  • See what personal information we hold about you
  • Correct anything that is wrong
  • Delete your information or remove you from our lists

Email dave@vote4yu.ca and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Questions

Contact us at dave@vote4yu.ca.
